DayZ + NVIDIA Freestyle / Game Filters
======================================

HARD LIMIT: Freestyle "Game Filters" on DayZ crash in ppeGetVersion
(NVIDIA PPE) during D3D init. Do not re-enable filters on DayZ.

Safe launch:
  C:\oak\dayz\LaunchDayZSafe.bat
  or: powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File C:\oak\dayz\LaunchDayZSafe.ps1 -InitSeconds 45

Steam Launch Options (quit Steam first, then edit):
  C:/oak/dayz/steam_wrap.cmd %command%

Overlay can return AFTER DayZ_x64 is stable. Applying Freestyle to DayZ
will crash again.

Color fallback (no Freestyle):
  NVIDIA Control Panel → Adjust desktop color settings → Digital Vibrance
